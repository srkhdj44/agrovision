How It Works

AgroVision follows a simple process from image upload to disease prediction.

  1. Upload Leaf Image

    Choose a clear photo of the crop leaf.

  2. Image Preprocessing

    The system resizes and prepares the image for the model.

  3. AI Prediction

    The trained deep learning model analyses the image.

  4. View Result

    See the predicted disease, confidence score, heatmap and guidance.
