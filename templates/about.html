About AgroVision

An AI-powered crop disease detection sytem

Purpose

To support early crop disease identification and help farmers take timely action for healthier yields.

What We Do

We analyse uploaded leaf images using AI and provide a predicted disease result with useful guidance.

Main Features

  • Upload a leaf image
  • Predict the disease class
  • View the confidence score
  • See which parts of the leaf the model focused on
  • Read disease information and guidance

Important Note

AgroVision provides AI-based prediction support only. Please consult an agricultural expert for confirmation.
