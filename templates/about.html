About AgroVision

An AI-powered crop disease detection system.

Purpose

To support early crop disease identification and help farmers take timely action for healthier yields.

What We Do

We analyse uploaded leaf images using AI and provide a predicted disease result with useful guidance.

Main Features

  • Upload a leaf image
  • Predict the disease class
  • View the confidence score
  • See other possible diseases
  • See which parts of the leaf the model focused on
  • Read disease information and guidance

Important Note

AgroVision gives AI-based predictions only. Please confirm with an agricultural expert.

The model was trained mostly on photos of single leaves on plain backgrounds, so it is less reliable on photos taken in the field.
