Disease Information

A common fungal disease of tomato and potato. It starts on the older, lower leaves and moves up the plant. Badly infected leaves turn brown and fall off.

Symptoms: Small dark spots on lower leaves that grow into round brown spots with target-like rings. The area around each spot often turns yellow.

Caused by: Fungus (Alternaria solani and Alternaria tomatophila)

Basic Guidance

  1. Remove leaves with spots and bury or burn them. Do not compost them.
  2. Water at the base of the plant and use mulch so soil does not splash onto the leaves.
  3. Space and stake plants so air can move between them.
  4. Rotate crops. Avoid planting tomato or potato in the same spot for two years.

Tips for a clear photo

  • Photograph one leaf, close up, filling most of the picture.
  • Use a plain background, such as a sheet of paper or your palm.
  • Take the photo in daylight, without strong shadows.
  • Make sure the leaf is in focus, not blurry.
