About this disease

A common fungal disease of tomato and potato. It starts on the older, lower leaves and moves up the plant.

Symptoms: Small dark spots that grow into round brown spots with target-like rings. The area around each spot often turns yellow.

Caused by: Fungus (Alternaria solani and Alternaria tomatophila)

What to do

  1. Remove leaves with spots and bury or burn them. Do not compost them.
  2. Water at the base of the plant and use mulch so soil does not splash onto the leaves.
  3. Space and stake plants so air can move between them.
  4. Rotate crops. Avoid planting tomato or potato in the same spot for two years.

Source: University of Minnesota Extension

About AgroVision

A final year project that helps identify crop leaf diseases from a photo.

Purpose

To support early identification of crop diseases, so farmers can act sooner.

What it does

  • Predicts the likely disease from a leaf photo
  • Shows a confidence score and other possibilities
  • Shows which parts of the leaf the model focused on
  • Gives disease information and basic guidance

Limits

The model was trained mostly on photos of single leaves on plain backgrounds. It is less reliable on photos taken in the field. AgroVision gives AI-based predictions only. Please confirm with an agricultural expert.
